01 — ABOUT ME

Not just ideas.
Things I can build.

Hey! I’m Omor TE. I love building new things, exploring technology, and turning random ideas into real projects. Whether it’s a website, a useful tool, or something connected to gaming, I enjoy learning how things work and finding ways to make them better.

I’m a huge fan of coding, video games, and anything creative that lets me experiment. Gaming is one of my favourite ways to unwind — especially titles like Modern Warships — while coding and building projects give me a chance to solve problems, learn something new, and bring weird, fun ideas to life. I also enjoy exploring AI tools, pixel art, web design, and retro-style visuals, and I’m always curious about how things are made under the hood.

I’m not here to pretend I know everything — I’m here to keep improving, try ideas that excite me, and build things people can actually use. This website is my little corner of the internet: a place to share what I make, what I’m interested in, and what I’m working toward.

Always learning. Always building. Always curious.
